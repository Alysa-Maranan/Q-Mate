HOW IT WORKS
------------

1. User triggers 10-second feed
   ↓
2. Animation starts
   - Record start time: startTime = Date.now()
   ↓
3. Update display every 100ms:
   - Calculate: elapsed = (Date.now() - startTime) / 1000
   - Calculate: remaining = 10 - elapsed
   - Calculate: percentage = (elapsed / 10) * 100
   - Update countdown, progress bar, percentage
   ↓
4. After EXACTLY 10 seconds:
   - setTimeout fires at 10000ms
   - Show 100% completion
   - Wait 1 second
   - Hide animation
   - Reload page
